License Summary

A simple, transparent guide to what you can and cannot do.

What is Allowed

  • Use audio in video games, Twitch streams, YouTube videos, and podcasts.
  • Use tracks in commercial and monetization-enabled projects.
  • Edit, remix, loop, and incorporate tracks into your creative works.
  • No royalties, recurring subscriptions, or renewal fees ever required.

What is NOT Allowed

  • Do not redistribute or sell raw music files on stock libraries or compilation albums.
  • Do not claim authorship of the original audio.
  • Do not register WhatsCR audio tracks with YouTube Content ID or automated copyright systems.
