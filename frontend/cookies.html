Cookie Policy

Complete disclosure on the use of cookies and local storage technologies.

1. Zero Profiling & No Advertising Cookies

WhatsCR respects user privacy and complies with the European ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC) and GDPR guidelines. We do not use any profiling, marketing, or behavioral tracking cookies, nor do we partner with third-party advertising networks that track you across websites.

2. Strictly Necessary Technical Cookies & Local Storage

WhatsCR only utilizes technical elements that are strictly required for the platform to function securely and efficiently:

  • Network Security: Essential security tokens provided by edge CDN services (Cloudflare) to defend against DDoS attacks, bot scraping, and request forgery.
  • Audio Player Preferences: Local storage settings in your browser to remember audio volume levels and mute states during your playback session.

Because these technologies are strictly technical and do not track personal identities or profiles, prior consent banners are not required by law.

3. Managing Cookie Settings

You can configure or block cookies at any time through your browser settings. Note that disabling strictly necessary security cookies may affect site connectivity or security features.

4. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding our cookie practices, please contact us at: whatscrorg@gmail.com.
