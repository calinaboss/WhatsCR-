About Us

What is WhatsCR and why we created it.

Our Mission

WhatsCR? (What's Copyright?) was built for creators, indie game developers, video editors, and digital artists who are tired of copyright claims, demonetized videos, and complicated licensing contracts.

We provide a curated library of high-quality sounds, music tracks, seamless game loops, and chiptune audio that anyone can download and use freely across commercial and non-commercial projects.

Zero Hassle Audio

Every track on WhatsCR is released under open licenses such as Creative Commons Zero (CC0) or dedicated safe commercial permissions. You do not need expensive subscriptions or permission to build your games, apps, or videos.

Community Driven

We are constantly expanding our library with audio tracks submitted by talented producers and creators worldwide. Our goal is to make copyright-free music accessible, transparent, and modern.
