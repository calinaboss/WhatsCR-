Digital Services Act (DSA) Compliance

Transparency information pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 of the European Parliament and of the Council.

Single Point of Contact for Authorities & Users (Articles 11 & 12 DSA)

In accordance with Articles 11 and 12 of the Digital Services Act, WhatsCR has established a single electronic point of contact for direct communications with Member States' authorities, the European Commission, the European Board for Digital Services, and users of the service:

Official Contact Email: whatscrorg@gmail.com

Communications to this point of contact may be submitted in English or Italian.

Intermediary Liability & No General Obligation to Monitor (Article 8 DSA)

As a hosting service provider, WhatsCR is subject to Article 8 of the DSA, which provides that hosting intermediaries have no general obligation to monitor information transmitted or stored, nor a general obligation to actively seek facts or circumstances indicating illegal activity. We act promptly to remove or disable access to illegal or infringing content upon receiving substantiated knowledge or notice.

Content Moderation & Redress Mechanisms (Article 16 DSA)

WhatsCR operates a transparent Notice-and-Action mechanism. Notices submitted by any individual or entity through our Report Content page are assessed manually in a timely, diligent, and objective manner. If content is removed, the submitter will receive written confirmation at the provided email address.
