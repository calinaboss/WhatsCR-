Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding our platform and music.

Can I use WhatsCR music in commercial games?

Yes. All music and loops published on WhatsCR can be used in commercial video games, mobile apps, Steam releases, and interactive projects without paying royalties.

Will I get a YouTube Content ID claim?

No. We strictly prohibit audio registered with YouTube Content ID or automated fingerprinting systems to guarantee zero strikes or demonetization for creators.

Do I have to give credit or attribution?

Giving attribution is appreciated and supports artists, but for tracks under CC0 Free license, attribution is not legally mandatory. Always check the specific badge on the track.

Can I re-sell the music as my own?

No. You cannot download audio from WhatsCR and sell the raw audio files or sound packs as your own standalone product.
