Privacy Policy

Full transparency on data protection and our commitment under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data Controller Identification

The Data Controller responsible for the processing of personal data on this platform under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) is the WhatsCR Project Administration.

Designated contact point for all privacy and data protection inquiries: .

2. Data Collection & Account Processing

WhatsCR operates on the principles of privacy-by-design and data minimization:

  • Browsing and downloading tracks is completely free and requires no account creation or personal details.
  • For users registering to upload audio tracks, we process solely an email address, display username, and cryptographically salted password hashes.
  • We never collect payment card information, banking details, or sensitive biometric identifiers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any external brokers or marketing aggregators.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies (Google AdSense)

To support server infrastructure and maintain free audio downloads, WhatsCR partners with Google AdSense to display advertisements:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other digital properties.
  • In the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland, advertising cookies and ad personalization are subject to prior user consent collected via a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP).
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or through the Digital Advertising Alliance Consumer Choice page.

4. Device Privacy & Zero Fingerprinting

WhatsCR enforces strict device protection standards:

  • We do not collect device serial numbers, battery status, bluetooth, USB, or hardware identifiers.
  • Our HTTP Permissions-Policy headers explicitly block browser access to device cameras, microphones, and geolocation sensors.
  • All uploaded creator images and avatars undergo automatic canvas stripping to permanently remove EXIF, GPS location coordinates, and camera metadata prior to storage.

5. Server Logs & Edge Infrastructure

When you browse or download files from WhatsCR, standard web infrastructure (provided by Cloudflare CDN edge nodes) automatically processes basic technical connection metadata strictly necessary for secure data transmission:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type, version, and operating system.
  • Date, time, and requested URL resource.

This technical data is processed pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (Legitimate Interest) solely to prevent distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, mitigate malicious traffic, and guarantee service uptime.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation, you are entitled to exercise the following rights:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR): Request confirmation whether your data is processed.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Restriction and Objection (Art. 18 & 21 GDPR): Object to processing on legitimate grounds.

To exercise any of these rights, please transmit your request to . We will respond within 30 days without undue delay.

7. Supervisory Authority

If you believe that the processing of your data violates European data protection regulations, you have the right to lodge a formal complaint with the competent supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali - garanteprivacy.it).
