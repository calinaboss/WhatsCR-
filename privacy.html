Privacy Policy

Full transparency on data protection and our commitment under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data Controller Identification

The Data Controller responsible for the processing of personal data on this platform under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) is the WhatsCR Project Administration.

Designated contact point for all privacy and data protection inquiries: .

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

WhatsCR is engineered around the principle of privacy-by-design and data minimization:

  • We do not require user account registration, login credentials, or passwords.
  • We do not collect credit card, payment, or financial details.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information under any circumstances.
  • We do not embed third-party advertising trackers or invasive behavioral tracking pixels.

3. Server Logs & Edge Infrastructure

When you browse or download files from WhatsCR, standard web infrastructure (provided by Cloudflare CDN edge nodes) automatically processes basic technical connection metadata strictly necessary for secure data transmission:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type, version, and operating system.
  • Date, time, and requested URL resource.

This technical data is processed pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (Legitimate Interest) solely to prevent distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, mitigate malicious traffic, and guarantee service uptime.

4. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation, you are entitled to exercise the following rights:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR): Request confirmation whether your data is processed.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Restriction and Objection (Art. 18 & 21 GDPR): Object to processing on legitimate grounds.

To exercise any of these rights, please transmit your request to . We will respond within 30 days without undue delay.

5. Supervisory Authority

If you believe that the processing of your data violates European data protection regulations, you have the right to lodge a formal complaint with the competent supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali - garanteprivacy.it).
