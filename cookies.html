Cookie Policy

Complete disclosure on the use of cookies, local storage technologies, and analytics.

1. Overview & Compliance

WhatsCR respects user privacy and complies with the European ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC), GDPR guidelines, and Google Consent Mode v2 standards. We provide transparent controls so you can decide which non-essential cookies to allow.

2. Strictly Necessary Technical Cookies

Technical cookies and local storage tokens are essential for the operation and security of the WhatsCR platform:

  • Edge Security & Defense: Security tokens from Cloudflare edge services to mitigate automated abuse, DDoS attacks, and cross-site forgery.
  • Audio Player State: Browser local storage entries saving volume settings and playback states.
  • Consent State: A local storage record saving your cookie choices so you are not prompted repeatedly.

Under applicable law, strictly necessary technical cookies do not require prior consent.

3. Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics 4)

With your explicit consent, WhatsCR utilizes Google Analytics (Measurement ID: G-NQY8JZWCJP) to collect aggregated, anonymous information regarding traffic, page views, and download counts. These insights help us understand which music genres and audio loops creators find most useful.

If you decline analytics cookies, Google Analytics operates in consent-denied mode without reading or storing identifiers on your device.

4. Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

With your consent, Google AdSense uses cookies to serve relevant advertisements that help support the server and bandwidth costs of offering free copyright-free audio. You can accept or deny advertising cookies at any time.

5. Updating Your Preferences

You can update your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the floating cookie icon at the bottom-left corner of any page, or using the management button above.
