Expedited Resolution Guarantee (24 to 48 Hours)

In accordance with the Safe Harbor provisions of the EU Digital Services Act (Regulation EU 2022/2065) and 17 U.S.C. § 512 (DMCA), all substantiated notices received at our designated address are reviewed and processed within 24 to 48 hours. Upon verification, the contested audio file will be immediately disabled or removed from public access.

Mandatory Information Required in Your Notice

To enable prompt verification, your formal notification must contain the following details:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work: The title, artist name, and a link or certificate verifying the original copyrighted material.
  • Specific location on WhatsCR: The exact URL link and track title of the allegedly infringing material on our platform.
  • Contact information: Your full legal name, company or organization (if applicable), mailing address, telephone number, and official email address.
  • Good faith statement: A statement that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • Statement under penalty of perjury: A statement confirming that the information provided in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  • Physical or electronic signature: An electronic signature (typed legal name) of the authorized person submitting the claim.
